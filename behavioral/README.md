# Behavioral

Forward Deployed Engineer behavioral interviews focus on ownership, comfort with ambiguity, customer empathy, and communication under pressure. Prepare a story bank and practice telling each story in two to three minutes.

## Themes to cover

- Owning a project end to end, including the messy parts.
- Working with a difficult stakeholder or customer.
- Making a call with incomplete information.
- Handling a failed pilot, missed deadline, or production incident.
- Influencing without authority.
- Learning a new domain quickly.
- Disagreeing with a teammate or manager and resolving it.
- Prioritizing when everything feels urgent.

## Frameworks

- **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep Situation and Task short, spend most time on Action, and quantify Result.
- **What I learned**: end each story with a one line takeaway.

## Story bank

Use [`../templates/story-bank-entry.md`](../templates/story-bank-entry.md) to capture each story. Keep entries in a private file if they contain sensitive details.
