# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment free space for everyone preparing for AI Forward Deployed Engineer roles, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good intent and give helpful feedback.
- Credit sources and respect the licenses of linked material.
- Keep shared interview content ethical: no leaked test questions under NDA, no paywalled material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private report by emailing the maintainer at ping.bhargav@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may block repeat offenders.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
