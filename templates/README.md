# Templates

Reusable templates for notes and writeups in this repository.

- [`case-study.md`](case-study.md): mock customer scenario and solution walkthrough.
- [`company-guide.md`](company-guide.md): company interview loop notes.
- [`story-bank-entry.md`](story-bank-entry.md): behavioral story in STAR format.
- [`journey-entry.md`](journey-entry.md): prep journey log entry.
- [`resource-note.md`](resource-note.md): summary of a book, course, or article.

Copy a template into the right folder and rename it with a lowercase, hyphenated filename.
