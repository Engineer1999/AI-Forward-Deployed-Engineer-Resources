# The Forward Deployed Engineer Craft

This folder covers the skills that make the Forward Deployed Engineer role distinct from a standard software or machine learning engineering job: working directly with customers to turn an ambiguous business problem into a deployed AI solution.

## What the role actually involves

- **Discovery**: sitting with customer teams, mapping their workflows, and finding where AI creates value.
- **Scoping**: defining a pilot with clear success metrics and a realistic timeline.
- **Building**: shipping integrations, data pipelines, and LLM features against a real stack.
- **Deploying**: getting the solution into the customer environment with security, access, and monitoring handled.
- **Driving adoption**: training users, gathering feedback, and iterating until the workflow sticks.
- **Expanding**: turning one successful pilot into more use cases.

## Skills to practice

- Stakeholder interviews and requirement gathering.
- Writing crisp problem statements and solution proposals.
- Estimating effort and communicating tradeoffs.
- Demoing to both executives and end users.
- Handling scope creep, blocked access, and shifting priorities.
- Knowing when to say no or to descope.

## Interview formats that test this

- **Customer scenario role play**: a mock stakeholder describes a vague problem and you scope it live.
- **Solution design**: you present how you would deliver a pilot for a given customer.
- **Behavioral deep dives**: ownership, ambiguity, and customer empathy stories.
